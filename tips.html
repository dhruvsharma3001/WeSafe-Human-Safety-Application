Essential Safety Tips

Empowering people with knowledge to stay safe in all environments. Follow these tips to ensure your safety and well-being.

1. Be Careful While Making Friends

Be cautious while making friends with any unknown person. He/She may cause you harm.

2. Avoid Easy Job Offers from Unknown Sources

Don’t get allured by easy job offers from unknown sources.

3. Dial 100 in Case of Emergency

Dial 100 in emergency situations to seek police help.

4. Avoid Meeting Unknown People in Unknown Places

Don’t meet any unknown person in any unknown place.

5. Don’t Assume Safety Based on Past Experiences

Don’t presume that because your area has been "safe" thus far, it will continue to be so.

6. Trust Your Instincts

Always listen to your instincts. Avoid a person or a situation that does not feel right.

7. Call Police if You Feel Threatened

If someone comes to your door that you do not trust or who you believe might be dangerous, call your local police department immediately.

8. Keep the House Well-Lit at Night

Keep the house well-lit at night to discourage intruders.

9. Ensure Doors and Windows are Properly Closed

Always ensure that all the doors and windows are properly closed before leaving the house.

10. Inform Relatives and Neighbors

Inform your relatives and neighbors that you are alone in the house for added safety.

11. Be Friendly Only When You Are Sure

Be friendly with anyone when you are 100% sure of them.

12. Share Your Plans

Inform others when and where you are going and check in with them when you reach your destination.

13. Report Robbery Attempts to the Police

If someone tries to rob you, report the crime to the police. Try to describe the attacker accurately to help prevent others from becoming victims.

14. Stay Alert on the Street

Wherever you are—on the street, in an office building, shopping mall, or driving—stay alert and tuned in to your surroundings.

15. Project Confidence

Send the message that you're calm, confident, and know where you're going.

16. Trust Your Instincts

If something or someone makes you uneasy, avoid the person or leave the situation.

17. Plan Your Journey

Think ahead and plan your journey, avoiding deserted areas in the dark. Always stick to well-lit areas.

18. Be Mindful if You’re Being Followed

If you think you are being followed, cross the road or change direction.

19. Stay Calm in Fearful Situations

If you start to feel frightened, try not to panic. Always think about how to handle the situation calmly.
